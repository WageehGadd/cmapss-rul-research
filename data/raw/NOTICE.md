# Third-party data notice

## Dataset

The `train_*`, `test_*` and `RUL_*` files (FD001-FD004) and `readme.txt` in this folder belong to the
NASA C-MAPSS turbofan engine degradation simulation data, published as the "Turbofan Engine Degradation
Simulation Data Set" and listed on data.gov as "CMAPSS Jet Engine Simulated Data".

The NASA repository page states that the data set was provided by the NASA Ames Prognostics Center of
Excellence (PCoE). The data are simulated with C-MAPSS (Commercial Modular Aero-Propulsion System
Simulation).

## Citation

> A. Saxena and K. Goebel (2008). "Turbofan Engine Degradation Simulation Data Set",
> NASA Prognostics Data Repository, NASA Ames Research Center.

The NASA repository asks publications that use its data to acknowledge both the repository and the
donors of the data. It also states that users employ the data at their own risk, and that neither NASA
nor the donors assume liability for its use.

The related paper, named in `readme.txt` and in the data.gov description: A. Saxena, K. Goebel,
D. Simon and N. Eklund, "Damage Propagation Modeling for Aircraft Engine Run-to-Failure Simulation",
Proceedings of the 1st International Conference on Prognostics and Health Management (PHM08),
Denver, CO, October 2008.
